## Piano-range law

Micro-slices are conceptual nodes visualized as musical notes. The piano is the inspirational reminder and correlation source.

Span: A-zero to C-eight. Eighty-eight keys. Each key is one micro-slice. Each octave mirrors the same twelve-note pattern upward.

Bass octave is the base — the foundation. Upper octaves are derivatives compounded on the same pitch classes. Octave equivalence: same class, higher register.

Fractionalization follows music: eighth, sixteenth, thirty-second notes and beyond. The rule is fixed — fractionalize first. The preference is depth on the note tree.

Zero is micro-slice. Check zero only when the presentment is uncut. Already cut means skip to the Boolean.

Coupling stage maps conceptual nodes to real notes on a staff with a time signature.
