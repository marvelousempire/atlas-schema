# Level 06 — Cassette

**Pad law:** Sealed data, zero logic. Content without behavior.

**WordPress synonym:** A plugin or theme file. Data that the console executes.

**Car-stack twin:** The fuel. Energy the engine consumes.

**Game-pool equivalent:** A saved game file. State without the engine.

**Coupler note:** Atlas is a cassette. It slots into any console. The ACT V4 canvas reads it.

**Civic rung:** Block, street. Your sealed unit of data.
