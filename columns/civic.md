# Column: Civic Ladder

Twelve rungs from place of abode to state. Concentric rings that collide.

1. Place of abode — your own residence. The origin.
2. Sporadic spots — one house in Jacksonville, one in Clearwater. Aunt's house. Scattered influence.
3. Street — a couple houses on your street.
4. Side of street — your direct neighbors, same side.
5. Whole block (two grids) — houses across the street. The across-the-street house is a different circular grid centered on itself.
6. Neighborhood — a couple blocks adjacent to each other.
7. Community — six blocks or more.
8. Zip code.
9. City.
10. County.
11. State.

The ladder is not a straight line. It is concentric rings starting to collide.
